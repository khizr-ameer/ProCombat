Process

How to Use Our Templates

Simple steps to go from template to production-ready custom gear.

01

Download the PDF

Click the Download PDF button for your product type. The file contains all panel dimensions, seam guides, and placement zones.

02

Place Your Design

Open in Illustrator, Photoshop, or your preferred design tool. Place your logo, artwork, and colour zones within the guides.

03

Send to ProCombat

Submit your completed design file via our contact form. We'll review, confirm specs, produce a sample, and begin your order.

Ready to Start Your Custom Order?

Download your templates, apply your design, and send it over. We'll handle the rest — from sample to bulk production.
