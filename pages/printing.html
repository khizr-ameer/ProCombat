Our Printing Techniques

Every technique explained — so you can choose what's right for your product.

Technique 01

Silk Screen Print

Silkscreen printing is among many ways to print designs onto an item. Due to its cloth-friendly nature, it is able to print solid-color designs onto just about anything, no matter the blend or processing of the material.

ProCombat employs negative ink techniques to create sharper, more detailed results. We are open to more than certain machines for printing complex designs — this allows for the best result in coverage and matching colors.

Technique 02

Puff Screen Printing

It uses specialised puff inks for screen printing t-shirts, sweatshirts, or jackets to give them a vintage appearance. This technique is prevalent and currently being applied to add a vintage look to sportswear.

Heat is applied to the image after ample ink has been transferred to achieve the puff effect. Images may require pure puffs, and some require Plastisol as an additive. A larger puff increases the risk of cracking — our team calibrates precisely.

3D Raised Effect Vintage Look T-shirts / Hoodies
Technique 03

Digital Printing

Digital printing is a valuable part of sports apparel manufacturing — providing a highly efficient method for individual athletes, teams, and clubs. It uses modern technology to create designs directly from computer files, dramatically reducing print costs and delivery times.

The resulting garment is lightweight, comfortable, durable, and built to withstand strenuous activity. Compared to sticker heat transfer, it is much more reliable with a significantly lower removal rate.

Photo-Quality Full Color Fast Delivery Low Cost

Technique 04

Flock Screen Printing

Synthetic fibres such as cotton, rayon, and polyester are the primary basis for flock. After applying an adhesive liquid to the designated area, the flock is applied to it.

Flock screen printing has an embossed and smooth texture. To achieve different colors, the first flock-coloured dye is used as a base, then layered on top of the adhesive through a screen. The result is a delicate velvety softness.

Velvet Texture Embossed Premium Look
Technique 05

Water-Based / Pigment Screen Printing

Due to the water-based nature of the color, pigment screen printing (also known as water-based screen printing) uses eco-friendly inks instead of Plastisol-based liquids. This technique is primarily employed on white and light-colored fabrics for a soft, breathable finish.

Eco-Friendly Inks Soft Hand Feel Light Fabrics

Discharge
Technique 06

Discharge Screen Print

The discharge screen printing technique uses different inks but the same machinery. Discharge inks replace the original color upon transfer, creating a fabric-like texture. They can be used alone or as a base for other inks, similar to water-based printing on darker fabrics.

High-quality, high-definition printing. Unlike puff printing which has soft characters and imperfect edges, discharge employs low-viscosity inks for sharper, finer edges and a subtle 3D effect — without the risk of cracking.

Dark Fabrics Sharp Edges Fabric Texture 3D Depth
