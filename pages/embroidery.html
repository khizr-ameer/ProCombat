Our Capabilities

Types of Embroidery We Offer

Choose from various types of embroidery available at our factory:

01

Flat Embroidery

Classic, clean stitching on any garment surface. Perfect for logos, text, and brand marks that need a professional finish.

02

3D Puff Embroidery

A raised, dimensional effect using foam underneath stitching. Gives logos a bold, premium, eye-catching appearance.

03

Appliqué Embroidery

Fabric pieces stitched onto the garment to create a layered, textured look with bold color areas and unique design depth.

04

Chain Stitch

A looped rope-like texture, often used for lettering and vintage-style branding with a distinctly artisan character.

05

Metallic & Specialty Threads

Gold, silver, and specialty threads for premium, eye-catching finishes. Perfect for high-end brand presentations.

06

Patch Embroidery

Custom embroidered patches that can be sewn, ironed, or velcro-attached. Ideal for team identification and brand recognition.

07

Cap & Headwear

Structured embroidery on caps and beanies with proper backing and stabilization for a clean, professional look.

08

All-Fabric Compatible

BJJ gis, MMA shorts, boxing gloves, rash guards, hoodies — our team is trained on every fabric type in combat sports.
